\begin{exercise}{141}
Let $p\colon K\to\mathcal C$ be a diagram. Suppose that there is a colimit cone $\tilde p$ of $p$ with
colimit $x$ in $\mathcal C$. Let $y$ be an object of $\mathcal C$ which is equivalent to $x$. Show that $y$ is also the colimit
of a colimit cone of $p$.

\begin{proof}
Tip evaluation $\pi\colon\mathcal C_{p/}\to\mathcal C$ is a left
fibration by Corollary~1.4.24(1).
Choose an equivalence $e\colon x\to y$ and lift it along $\pi$ to an
edge $h\colon\tilde p\to\tilde q$ starting at $\tilde p$.
Proposition~2.1.7 makes $h$ an equivalence.
By Theorem~4.3.11, $\tilde p$ is initial in $\mathcal C_{p/}$;
Exercise~\ref{ex:136} therefore makes $\tilde q$ initial as well.
Theorem~4.3.11 now makes $\tilde q$ a colimit cone of $p$,
and its tip is $\pi(\tilde q)=y$.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
