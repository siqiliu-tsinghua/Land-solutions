\begin{exercise}{1}
Let $h(\mathrm{CW})$ be the homotopy category of CW-complexes. Show
that this category does not have all pushouts. More concretely, show
that the diagram
\[
 \ast\longleftarrow S^1\xrightarrow{\,\cdot 2\,}S^1
\]
does not admit a pushout.

\begin{proof}
Suppose a pushout $P$ exists, and represent its structure map by
$u\colon S^1\to P$. Write $[A,B]$ for unbased homotopy classes of maps.
The universal property implies that $[P,S^2]$ is a singleton, since
both $[*,S^2]$ and $[S^1,S^2]$ are singletons.

Let $R=\mathbb{RP}^2=S^1\cup_2 D^2$, with inclusion $j\colon S^1\to R$.
Since $u\circ 2$ is nullhomotopic, $u$ extends to a map $v\colon R\to P$.
Since $j\circ 2$ is nullhomotopic, the pushout property also gives
$w\colon P\to R$ with $wu\simeq j$. Thus $f=wv$ satisfies $fj\simeq j$.

Use cohomology with $\mathbb F_2$ coefficients. Recall that
$H^*(R;\mathbb F_2)=\mathbb F_2[t]/(t^3)$, where $|t|=1$.\footnote{See
A.~Hatcher, \emph{Algebraic Topology}, Theorem~3.19, p.~220;
\href{https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/ATch3.pdf}{author's online edition}.}
Cellular cohomology shows that $j^*$ is an isomorphism in degree~$1$,
and that the quotient $q\colon R\to R/S^1\cong S^2$ induces an
isomorphism in degree~$2$. From $fj\simeq j$ we get $f^*(t)=t$, hence
$f^*(t^2)=t^2\ne0$. Therefore $(qf)^*=f^*q^*$ is nonzero in degree~$2$.
But $qf=(qw)v$ is nullhomotopic because $[P,S^2]$ is a singleton.
This contradiction proves the claim.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
