\begin{exercise}{98}
Let $\mathcal C$ be an $\infty$-category and let
$\mathcal C\to\mathcal S(|\mathcal C|)$ be the unit map of the adjunction
$(\mathcal S,|{-}|)$. Show that this is a localization of $\mathcal C$ along
all morphisms.

\begin{proof}
Put $K=\mathcal S(|\mathcal C|)$ and write $\eta\colon\mathcal C\to K$
for the unit. By Lemma~2.4.6, choose an anodyne localization
$j\colon\mathcal C\to L$ along all morphisms, with $L$ Kan.
Also $K$ is Kan by Lemma~1.1.47, and $\eta$ is anodyne by
Exercise~\ref{ex:97}.
Lifting against $K\to\Delta^0$ and $L\to\Delta^0$ gives maps
$\varphi\colon L\to K$ and $\psi\colon K\to L$ satisfying
\[
 \varphi j=\eta,\qquad \psi\eta=j.
\]
Theorem~1.3.37 gives trivial fibrations
\[
 \mathrm{Fun}(L,L)\longrightarrow\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal C,L),
 \qquad
 \mathrm{Fun}(K,K)\longrightarrow\mathrm{Fun}(\mathcal C,K).
\]
The maps $\psi\varphi$ and $\mathrm{id}_L$ have the same restriction
along $j$, and $\varphi\psi$ and $\mathrm{id}_K$ have the same restriction
along $\eta$.
The two trivial fibrations therefore supply homotopies between these pairs.
Their source functor categories are Kan by Corollary~1.3.38,
so the homotopies are natural equivalences.
Thus $\varphi$ is a Joyal equivalence with inverse $\psi$.

For every $\infty$-category $\mathcal D$, restriction along $\eta$ factors as
\[
 \mathrm{Fun}(K,\mathcal D)\xrightarrow{\varphi^*}
 \mathrm{Fun}(L,\mathcal D)\xrightarrow{j^*}
 \mathrm{Fun}^{\simeq}(\mathcal C,\mathcal D).
\]
Both factors are Joyal equivalences, by Proposition~2.2.8 and
Remark~2.4.3, respectively.
This is the localization property of Definition~2.4.2.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
