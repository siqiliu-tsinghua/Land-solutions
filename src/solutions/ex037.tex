\begin{exercise}{37}
Let $F\colon\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ be a functor with the right adjoint
$G\colon\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$. Show that they are mutually inverse if $F$ is fully faithful
and $G$ is conservative. Here, conservativity means that if $f\colon x\to y$ is a morphism
in $\mathcal D$ and $G(f)$ is an isomorphism, then $f$ is an isomorphism.

\begin{proof}
Let $\eta\colon\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal C}\to GF$ and
$\varepsilon\colon FG\to\mathrm{id}_{\mathcal D}$ be the unit
and counit. By Lemma~1.2.19(2), full faithfulness of $F$ makes
$\eta$ an isomorphism. For every $d\in\mathcal D$, the triangle
identity of Exercise~\ref{ex:9} gives
\[
 G(\varepsilon_d)\eta_{Gd}=\mathrm{id}_{Gd},
 \qquad G(\varepsilon_d)=\eta_{Gd}^{-1}.
\]
Thus $G(\varepsilon_d)$ is an isomorphism. Conservativity of
$G$ implies that $\varepsilon_d$ is an isomorphism as well.
Hence both unit and counit are natural isomorphisms, exhibiting
$F$ and $G$ as mutual quasi-inverses.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
