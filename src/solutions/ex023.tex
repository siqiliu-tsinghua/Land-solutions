\begin{exercise}{23}
Let $X$ be a composer and let $f\colon x\to y$ be a morphism in $X$.
Show that $f$ is a composition of $\mathrm{id}_x$ with $f$ and of
$f$ with $\mathrm{id}_y$.

\begin{proof}
The simplicial identities of Exercise~\ref{ex:2} give
\[
 \begin{array}{c|ccc}
       & d_2 & d_0 & d_1 \\
  s_0f & s_0d_1f=\mathrm{id}_x & f & f \\
  s_1f & f & s_0d_0f=\mathrm{id}_y & f
 \end{array}
\]
For a $2$-simplex, $d_2$ and $d_0$ are its two successive edges,
and $d_1$ is its edge from vertex $0$ to vertex $2$.
Thus $s_0f$ extends the spine $(\mathrm{id}_x,f)$ and $s_1f$
extends $(f,\mathrm{id}_y)$, both with long edge $f$.
By Definition~1.2.2 these are the two required compositions.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
