\begin{exercise}{26}
Determine the homotopy category of the following simplicial sets:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,
                  topsep=.5\baselineskip]
 \item $\partial\Delta^n$ for $n\ge1$
 \item $\Lambda_j^n$ for $n\ge2$ and $0\le j\le n$
 \item $I^n$ for $n\ge0$
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proof}
By Definition~1.2.5, $hX$ is generated by the nondegenerate edges,
with the relation $a_{ik}=a_{jk}a_{ij}$ for each nondegenerate
triangle $(i,j,k)$; degenerate triangles impose only the unit
relations. Here $a_{ij}$ denotes the edge $i\to j$.
If $X\subseteq\Delta^n$ contains the full $2$-skeleton, these
relations reduce every path to its endpoint edge, giving
$hX\cong[n]$, in agreement with Lemma~1.2.7.

(1) For $n=1$ the answer is the discrete category on $0,1$.
For $n=2$ it is the free category on the directed triangle
$0\to1\to2$, $0\to2$; in particular its two morphisms $0\to2$
are the edge and the composite. For $n\ge3$ the full
$2$-skeleton of $\Delta^n$ is present, so the answer is $[n]$.

(2) For $n=2$ there are no nondegenerate triangles. The answers
for $j=0,1,2$ are respectively the free categories on
\[
 1\longleftarrow0\longrightarrow2,
 \qquad 0\longrightarrow1\longrightarrow2,
 \qquad 0\longrightarrow2\longleftarrow1.
\]
The middle category is $[2]$.

For $n=3$, write
$a=a_{01}$, $b=a_{12}$, $c=a_{23}$, $d=a_{02}$,
$e=a_{13}$ and $f=a_{03}$.
The four triangle relations, in order of the omitted vertex, are
\[
 e=cb,\qquad f=cd,\qquad f=ea,\qquad d=ba.
\]
The horn $\Lambda_j^3$ omits exactly relation $j$.
For $j=1,2$, the missing relation follows from the other three
by associativity, so $h(\Lambda_j^3)\cong[3]$.
For $j=0$, eliminate $d,f$ to obtain the free category on
$a,b,c,e$ subject only to $ea=cba$.
This relation only affects paths from $0$ to $3$, so
$\operatorname{Hom}(1,3)=\{e,cb\}$ has two distinct elements;
all other hom-sets are as in $[3]$. Both elements become equal
after precomposition with $a$.
For $j=3$, eliminating $e,f$ instead leaves the generators
$a,b,c,d$ and the relation $cd=cba$.
Thus $\operatorname{Hom}(0,2)=\{d,ba\}$ has two distinct
elements, which become equal after postcomposition with $c$;
all other hom-sets are as in $[3]$. These descriptions specify
the compositions as well as the hom-sets.

For $n\ge4$, every $2$-simplex of $\Delta^n$ omits at least
two vertices, hence one different from $j$. Thus the horn
contains the full $2$-skeleton, and $h(\Lambda_j^n)\cong[n]$.

(3) The spine has only the nondegenerate edges
$0\to1\to\cdots\to n$ and no nondegenerate triangles.
Its homotopy category is the free category on this directed
line, namely $[n]$, also for $n=0$.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
