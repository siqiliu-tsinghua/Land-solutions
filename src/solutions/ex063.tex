\begin{exercise}{63}
Show that a trivial fibration $f\colon X\to Y$ between Kan complexes induces
an isomorphism in the category $\pi(\mathrm{Kan})$. Hint: Show that a trivial fibration between Kan
complexes is a homotopy equivalence.

\begin{proof}
By Definition~1.3.24 and Exercise~\ref{ex:59}, $f$ has the
RLP with respect to every monomorphism.
Lifting against $\varnothing\hookrightarrow Y$, with bottom
map $\mathrm{id}_Y$, gives a section $s\colon Y\to X$,
so $fs=\mathrm{id}_Y$.
Now consider
\[
 \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=large,row sep=large]
  X\times\partial\Delta^1
    \arrow[r,"{(\mathrm{id}_X,sf)}"] \arrow[d,hook]
    & X\arrow[d,"f"] \\
  X\times\Delta^1\arrow[r,"f\operatorname{pr}_X"']
    \arrow[ur,dashed,"H"] & Y .
 \end{tikzcd}
\]
The square commutes since $fs=\mathrm{id}_Y$, and its left
map is a monomorphism, so a lift $H$ exists.
It is a homotopy from $\mathrm{id}_X$ to $sf$
(Definition~1.1.50).
Together with $fs=\mathrm{id}_Y$, this makes $s$ a homotopy
inverse of $f$.
Thus $[s][f]=[\mathrm{id}_X]$ and
$[f][s]=[\mathrm{id}_Y]$ in $\pi(\mathrm{Kan})$,
so $[f]$ is an isomorphism.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
