\begin{exercise}{86}
Show that the class of essentially surjective and fully faithful functors satisfies
the 3-for-2 property.

\begin{proof}
By Proposition~2.3.5 and Theorem~2.3.20, this class is
exactly the class of Joyal equivalences.
By Definitions~2.1.14 and~1.2.22, these are the functors
whose classes in $h\mathrm{Cat}_\infty$ are isomorphisms.
For composable functors $F,G$, the triangle with long edge
$GF$ and constant composition witness gives
$[GF]=[G][F]$ in this category
(Definition~2.1.13).
The 3-for-2 property for isomorphisms therefore proves
the assertion.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
