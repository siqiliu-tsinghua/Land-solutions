\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
This volume is a companion to Markus Land's
\emph{Introduction to Infinity-Categories} (2021), containing solutions
to all 157 exercises. It grew out of an experiment asking whether an
orchestrated team of language models could do mathematical research.
GLM agents worked in swarms, exploring problems from several angles
and accumulating facts and their dependencies in directed graphs.
When progress stalled, the system could consult GPT or Gemini.

After development, Land's exercises served as a benchmark for the
system. On the harder problems in this experiment, the swarm of models
of moderate capability still fell short of the strongest single model
tested. Later attempts at open research problems produced no solutions,
and the project was abandoned.

GPT subsequently recovered the Land answers, checked their arguments,
and reorganized and simplified them into the present volume. Usable
earlier proofs and constructions were retained, while gaps, incorrect
assumptions, and mistaken statements were repaired. GPT's retrospective
estimate, with each exercise weighted equally, assigns contributions
of about $60:32:8$ to GLM, GPT, and Gemini.

Exercise statements retain the book's wording, numbering, and
mathematical layout. Necessary corrections are explained in local
footnotes, with counterexamples or corrected assertions where needed.
The solutions follow the book's order and use earlier exercises where
doing so avoids repeating proofs or introducing unnecessary background.
The text errata in the appendix were compiled by GLM.
References to numbered definitions, lemmas, propositions, and theorems
refer to Land's book unless stated otherwise. Products of maps are
composed from right to left, and $\alpha^*$ denotes $X(\alpha)$ for a
simplicial set $X$.
