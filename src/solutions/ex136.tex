\begin{exercise}{136}
Let $\mathcal C$ be an $\infty$-category and $x$ an initial object of $\mathcal C$. Show that if $y$ is
equivalent to $x$, then $y$ is also initial in $\mathcal C$.

\begin{proof}
Choose an equivalence $e\colon y\to x$.
The unit, associativity and inverse homotopies in the proof of
Exercise~\ref{ex:64} apply equally to precomposition. Hence, for every
object $z$, they give a homotopy equivalence
\[
 e^*\colon\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal C}(x,z)
 \longrightarrow\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal C}(y,z).
\]
Its source is contractible because $x$ is initial, so its target is
contractible as well. Definition~4.1.1 therefore makes $y$ initial.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
