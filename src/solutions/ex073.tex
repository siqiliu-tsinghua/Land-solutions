\begin{exercise}{73}
Show that for an object $x$ of a general simplicial set $X$, the canonical map
$X_{x/}\to X$ is generally not a left fibration.

\begin{proof}
Take $X=I^2=\Delta^{\{0,1\}}\cup\Delta^{\{1,2\}}$
and $x=0$.
By the join model (Definition~1.4.13 and
Exercise~\ref{ex:68}), a vertex of $X_{x/}$ over $v$
is an edge $0\to v$ in $X$.
Thus the edge $01$ gives a vertex $a$ over $1$, whereas
there is no vertex over $2$, since $02$ is absent.
Now take the lifting problem with left map
$\Lambda^1_0=\{0\}\hookrightarrow\Delta^1$, top map
selecting $a$, and bottom map the edge $12$ in $X$.
It commutes because the source of $12$ is the image of
$a$, namely $1$.
A lift would send the final vertex of $\Delta^1$ to a
vertex of $X_{x/}$ over $2$, which does not exist.
Hence the projection fails the left-horn lifting
condition of Definition~1.3.1.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
