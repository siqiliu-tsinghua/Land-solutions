\begin{exercise}{139}
Show that there exists a simplicial set $X$ such that map $\mathrm{Tw}(X)\to X\times X^{\mathrm{op}}$
of Definition~4.2.3 is not a right fibration.

\begin{proof}
Take $X=\Lambda^2_1$, with edges $a\colon0\to1$ and $b\colon1\to2$,
and denote the given map by $p$.
The vertices of $\mathrm{Tw}(X)$ are the edges of $X$, projected to
their ordered pairs of endpoints; in particular, $p(a)=(0,1)$.
The pair $(\mathrm{id}_0,b^{\mathrm{op}})$ defines an edge
$(0,2)\to(0,1)$ in $X\times X^{\mathrm{op}}$.
A lift of this edge with target $a$ would have a source vertex of
$\mathrm{Tw}(X)$ over $(0,2)$, hence an edge $0\to2$ in $X$.
There is no such edge.
Thus the lifting problem for $\Lambda^1_1=\{1\}\hookrightarrow\Delta^1$
with top vertex $a$ has no solution, and $p$ is not a right fibration.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
