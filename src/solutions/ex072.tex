\begin{exercise}{72}
For an object $x$ in an $\infty$-category $\mathcal C$, show that the canonical map $\mathcal C_{x/}\to\mathcal C$ is a
left fibration and that $\mathcal C_{/x}\to\mathcal C$ is a right fibration.

\begin{proof}
Apply Corollary~1.4.24(1) to
\[
 \varnothing\hookrightarrow\Delta^0
 \xrightarrow{x}\mathcal C\longrightarrow\Delta^0.
\]
The first map is a monomorphism, and the last is an
inner fibration because $\mathcal C$ is an
$\infty$-category.
Its $A=\varnothing$ clause gives exactly the canonical
projections $\mathcal C_{x/}\to\mathcal C$ and
$\mathcal C_{/x}\to\mathcal C$ as a left and a right
fibration, respectively.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
