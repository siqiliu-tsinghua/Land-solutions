\begin{exercise}{65}
Show that that composition as defined right before Definition~1.3.47 is associative up to homotopy, i.e., that composition in an $\infty$-category determines a category enriched
in $h(\mathrm{Kan})$, the homotopy category of Kan complexes. Hint: Consider the diagram
\[
 \begin{tikzcd}[column sep=small,row sep=large]
  \Delta^{\{0,1\}}\cup\Delta^{\{1,2\}}\cup\Delta^{\{2,3\}}
    \arrow[r,"\star"] \arrow[d,"\star"']
    & \Delta^{\{0,1,2\}}\cup\Delta^{\{2,3\}}
      \arrow[d,"\star"]
    & \Delta^{\{0,2\}}\cup\Delta^{\{2,3\}}
      \arrow[l] \arrow[d,"\star"] \\
  \Delta^{\{0,1\}}\cup\Delta^{\{1,2,3\}}
    \arrow[r,"\star"]
    & \Delta^3
    & \Delta^{\{0,2,3\}} \arrow[l] \\
  \Delta^{\{0,1\}}\cup\Delta^{\{1,3\}}
    \arrow[r,"\star"] \arrow[u]
    & \Delta^{\{0,1,3\}} \arrow[u]
    & \Delta^{\{0,3\}} \arrow[l] \arrow[u]
 \end{tikzcd}
\]
and show that all maps labelled with a $\star$ are inner-anodyne.

\begin{proof}
Abbreviate the face on vertices $a,b,c$ by
$\Delta^{abc}$, and likewise for edges.
Four starred maps are pushouts of
$\Lambda^2_1\hookrightarrow\Delta^2$:
the two starred arrows out of $I^3$ attach the faces
$012$ and $123$ along $01\cup12$ and $12\cup23$,
respectively; the starred arrows into $\Delta^{023}$ and
$\Delta^{013}$ attach those faces along $02\cup23$ and
$01\cup13$.
For the remaining two, factor the maps through
\[
\begin{aligned}
 \Delta^{012}\cup\Delta^{23}
   &\hookrightarrow \Delta^{012}\cup\Delta^{023}
     \hookrightarrow\Delta^3,\\
 \Delta^{01}\cup\Delta^{123}
   &\hookrightarrow \Delta^{123}\cup\Delta^{013}
     \hookrightarrow\Delta^3.
\end{aligned}
\]
Each first map attaches a triangle along its inner
$2$-horn; each second map is inner-anodyne by the
two-face computation in Exercise~\ref{ex:64}.
Closure under pushouts and finite composition
(Corollary~1.3.16) proves the assertion for all six stars.

Take the objects of $\mathcal C$, give $(x,y)$ the
hom-object represented by
$\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal C}(x,y)$ in $h(\mathrm{Kan})$,
and use the chosen composition maps and the degenerate
identity edges for composition and units.
The full three-variable associativity homotopy and both
unit homotopies were proved in Exercise~\ref{ex:64}.
Their images in $h(\mathrm{Kan})$ are equalities, so these
data satisfy the enriched associativity and unit axioms.
The comparison of composition choices there also shows
that their homotopy classes are independent of the
chosen section.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
