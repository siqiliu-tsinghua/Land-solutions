\begin{exercise}{118}
Let $p\colon X\to S$ be an inner fibration of simplicial sets and $f\colon x\to y$ an edge
in $X$. Suppose that $f$ is $p'$-cocartesian, where $p'$ is given by the following pullback:
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
 X' \arrow[r] \arrow[d,"p'"'] & X \arrow[d,"p"] \\
 S' \arrow[r] & S
\end{tikzcd}
\]
Show that $f$ is locally cocartesian.

\begin{proof}
Write $f'\colon\Delta^1\to X'$ for the assumed $p'$-cocartesian edge
projecting to $f$, and put $\gamma'=p'f'$.
Pull $p'$ back along $\gamma'\colon\Delta^1\to S'$.
The outer-horn argument in Exercise~\ref{ex:117} applies to any inner
fibration and shows that the edge $(f',\mathrm{id}_{\Delta^1})$
is cocartesian for this pullback projection.

If $\kappa\colon S'\to S$ is the given map, then $\kappa\gamma'=pf$.
Pullback pasting gives an isomorphism over $\Delta^1$
\[
 X'\times_{S'}\Delta^1
 \cong (X\times_S S')\times_{S'}\Delta^1
 \cong X\times_S\Delta^1,
\]
where the last pullback uses $pf\colon\Delta^1\to S$.
It sends $(f',\mathrm{id}_{\Delta^1})$ to $(f,\mathrm{id}_{\Delta^1})$
and preserves the outer-horn lifting property.
Thus this last edge is cocartesian for $X\times_S\Delta^1\to\Delta^1$,
which is exactly Definition~3.2.5 of $f$ being locally cocartesian.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
