\begin{exercise}{43}
Prove or disprove the following statements:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),leftmargin=*,itemsep=0pt,parsep=0pt,
                  topsep=.5\baselineskip]
 \item There exists a simplicial category $\mathcal C$ whose coherent nerve $\mathrm{N}(\mathcal C)$ is not an $\infty$-category.
 \item There exists a simplicial category $\mathcal C$ whose coherent nerve $\mathrm{N}(\mathcal C)$ is an $\infty$-category, but
 not a Kan complex.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proof}
Both statements are true.

(1) Put $K=\Lambda^2_1\subseteq\Delta^2$.
Pointwise minimum makes $K$ a simplicial monoid with unit
the vertex $2$. Indeed, if either input misses $2$, so does
their minimum; otherwise both inputs miss $0$, as does their
minimum. This operation commutes with precomposition and
is associative and unital.
Let $\mathcal B$ be the one-object simplicial category with
endomorphism object $K$ and this composition.
Write $e_a$ for the edge of $\mathrm{N}(\mathcal B)$ corresponding
to $a\in K_0=\{0,1,2\}$.
By Definition~1.2.56, a triangle with successive edges
$e_a,e_b$ and long edge $e_c$ amounts to an edge
$c\to\min(a,b)$ in $K$.

Define three triangles on the faces indicated below:
\[
 \begin{array}{c|c|c|c}
  \text{face}&(a,b)&c&\text{edge in }K\\
  (123)&(2,2)&1&1\to2\\
  (013)&(2,1)&0&0\to1\\
  (012)&(2,2)&2&2\to2
 \end{array}
\]
They agree on the shared edges $(13),(12),(01)$, with
values $e_1,e_2,e_2$, respectively, and hence define
$\Lambda^3_1\to\mathrm{N}(\mathcal B)$.
A filler would supply the missing face $(023)$, whose
successive edges are $e_2,e_2$ and whose long edge is $e_0$.
This would require an edge $0\to2$ in $K$, which does not exist.
Thus $\mathrm{N}(\mathcal B)$ is not an $\infty$-category.

(2) Take $\mathcal C=c[1]$. By Lemma~1.2.64,
$\mathrm{N}(\mathcal C)\cong\mathrm{N}([1])\cong\Delta^1$.
As the nerve of a category, this is an $\infty$-category by
Theorem~1.1.52.
The outer horn $\Lambda^2_0$ maps to $\Delta^1$ with vertex
values $(0,1,0)$: its edge $(01)$ maps to $0\to1$ and
its edge $(02)$ is constant at $0$.
It has no filler, since a $2$-simplex of $\Delta^1$ must have
a nondecreasing vertex list. Hence it is not a Kan complex.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
