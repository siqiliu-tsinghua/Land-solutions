\begin{exercise}{69}
The functors $X\star-$ and $-\star X$ as functors $\mathrm{sSet}\to\mathrm{sSet}$ preserve pushouts. Find
an example of a colimit that is not preserved by $X\star-$.

\begin{proof}
Definition~1.4.8 gives a decomposition, natural in $Y$,
\[
 (X\star Y)_n
 =X_n\amalg Y_n\amalg
   \coprod_{\substack{p+q=n-1\\p,q\ge0}}X_p\times Y_q.
\]
For maps in $Y$, each displayed summand is preserved.
Thus the pushout of a span of these sets splits into
the pushouts of its summands.
The constant $X_n$ summands are glued along identity
maps, giving $X_n$ again.
Every other summand preserves pushouts: evaluation of
simplicial sets does, and taking a product with a fixed
set does.
Since colimits of simplicial sets are computed degreewise
(Proposition~1.1.23), $X\star-$ preserves pushouts.
The same argument applied to
\[
 (Y\star X)_n
 =Y_n\amalg X_n\amalg
   \coprod_{\substack{p+q=n-1\\p,q\ge0}}Y_p\times X_q
\]
proves the assertion for $-\star X$.

For the counterexample, take $X=\Delta^0$ and the empty
diagram.
Its colimit is $\varnothing$, whereas
$\Delta^0\star\varnothing\cong\Delta^0$:
in the cut formula, only $([n],\varnothing)$ contributes
in degree $n$, and these identifications respect all
structure maps.
Hence $X\star-$ does not preserve this colimit.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
