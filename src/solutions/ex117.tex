\begin{exercise}{117}
Suppose that you are given a pullback diagram
\[
\begin{tikzcd}
 X' \arrow[r] \arrow[d,"p'"'] & X \arrow[d,"p"] \\
 Y' \arrow[r] & Y
\end{tikzcd}
\]
in which the map $p$ is a (co)cartesian fibration. Show that the map $p'$ is a (co)cartesian
fibration as well.

\begin{proof}
Identify $X'$ with $X\times_Y Y'$.
Since inner fibrations are stable under pullback, $p'$ is an inner fibration.
An edge $e'$ of $X'$ whose image $e$ in $X$ is $p$-(co)cartesian
is $p'$-(co)cartesian: project any outer-horn problem of
Definition~3.1.1 to $X\to Y$, fill it there using $e$, and pair this
filler with the prescribed simplex $\Delta^n\to Y'$.
The pair lies in $X'$ and agrees with the original horn after both projections,
so it is the required filler.

Now let $\gamma\colon\Delta^1\to Y'$ be an edge with a specified lift
of its source in the cocartesian case, or of its target in the cartesian case.
Lift its image in $Y$ to a $p$-(co)cartesian edge $e$ with that endpoint in $X$.
The pair $(e,\gamma)$ is an edge of $X'$ with the prescribed endpoint.
By the preceding argument it is $p'$-(co)cartesian.
This verifies Definition~3.1.20 for $p'$ in both cases.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
