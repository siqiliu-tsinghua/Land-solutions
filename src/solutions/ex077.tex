\begin{exercise}{77}
Show that a functor $F\colon\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ between $\infty$-categories is
conservative if and only if the induced functor $hF\colon h\mathcal C\to h\mathcal D$
between the homotopy categories is conservative. Furthermore, show that the canonical
functor $\mathcal C\to\mathrm N(h\mathcal C)$ is conservative.

\begin{proof}
Every morphism of $h\mathcal C$ has the form $[f]$ for an edge
$f$ of $\mathcal C$, by Corollary~1.2.13, and
$hF([f])=[F(f)]$.
By Definition~1.2.22, $f$ is an equivalence exactly when $[f]$
is an isomorphism, and the same applies to $F(f)$ in $\mathcal D$.
Thus $F$ reflects equivalences exactly when $hF$ reflects
isomorphisms, proving the first assertion.

The canonical functor $\eta_{\mathcal C}\colon\mathcal C\to
\mathrm N(h\mathcal C)$ is the unit of the adjunction
$h\dashv\mathrm N$ from Proposition~1.2.18.
Its counit $\varepsilon_{h\mathcal C}\colon
h\mathrm N(h\mathcal C)\to h\mathcal C$ is the isomorphism
of Corollary~1.2.14.
The triangle identity
$\varepsilon_{h\mathcal C}\circ h(\eta_{\mathcal C})
=\mathrm{id}_{h\mathcal C}$ therefore makes
$h(\eta_{\mathcal C})$ an isomorphism of categories.
It is conservative, so the first assertion shows that
$\eta_{\mathcal C}$ is conservative as well.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
