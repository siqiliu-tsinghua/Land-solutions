\begin{exercise}{39}
Show that the left adjoint of a monoidal adjunction is in fact monoidal. Recall
that a monoidal adjunction consists of lax monoidal functors $F$ and $G$, which are witnessed
to be adjoint by a unit $\eta$ and a counit $\varepsilon$ where both $\eta$ and $\varepsilon$ are monoidal transformations.

\begin{proof}
Write $\nu,\nu_0$ and $\mu,\mu_0$ for the given lax structures
on $F\colon\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ and $G\colon\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$.
By Definition~1.2.32, monoidality of $\eta,\varepsilon$ means
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  G(\nu_{X,Y})\mu_{FX,FY}(\eta_X\otimes\eta_Y)
   &=\eta_{X\otimes Y},\\
  G(\nu_0)\mu_0&=\eta_{\bbone_{\mathcal C}},\\
  \varepsilon_{A\otimes B}F(\mu_{A,B})\nu_{GA,GB}
   &=\varepsilon_A\otimes\varepsilon_B,\\
  \varepsilon_{\bbone_{\mathcal D}}F(\mu_0)\nu_0
   &=\mathrm{id}_{\bbone_{\mathcal D}}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Let $\delta,\delta_0$ be the oplax structure on $F$ induced
from $\mu,\mu_0$ in Exercise~\ref{ex:38}.
By \eqref{eq:monoidal-mates} and the first equation above,
\[
 G(\nu_{X,Y}\delta_{X,Y})\eta_{X\otimes Y}
 =G(\nu_{X,Y})\mu_{FX,FY}(\eta_X\otimes\eta_Y)
 =\eta_{X\otimes Y}.
\]
Injectivity of transposition gives
$\nu_{X,Y}\delta_{X,Y}=\mathrm{id}_{F(X\otimes Y)}$.
For the other composite, the defining formula for $\delta$
and naturality of $\nu$ give
\[
 \begin{aligned}
  \delta_{X,Y}\nu_{X,Y}
   &=\varepsilon_{FX\otimes FY}F(\mu_{FX,FY})
      \nu_{GFX,GFY}\bigl(F(\eta_X)\otimes F(\eta_Y)\bigr)\\
   &=(\varepsilon_{FX}\otimes\varepsilon_{FY})
      \bigl(F(\eta_X)\otimes F(\eta_Y)\bigr)\\
   &=\mathrm{id}_{FX\otimes FY}.
 \end{aligned}
\]
Here the second equality uses monoidality of $\varepsilon$,
and the last uses the triangle identities.

For the unit maps, \eqref{eq:monoidal-mates} and monoidality
of $\varepsilon$ give
\[
 \delta_0\nu_0
 =\varepsilon_{\bbone_{\mathcal D}}F(\mu_0)\nu_0
 =\mathrm{id}_{\bbone_{\mathcal D}}.
\]
Likewise,
$G(\nu_0\delta_0)\eta_{\bbone_{\mathcal C}}
=G(\nu_0)\mu_0=\eta_{\bbone_{\mathcal C}}$,
so transposition gives
$\nu_0\delta_0=\mathrm{id}_{F(\bbone_{\mathcal C})}$.
Thus the given lax structure maps have inverses
$\delta,\delta_0$, and $F$ is monoidal by Definition~1.2.31.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
