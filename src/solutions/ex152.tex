\begin{exercise}{152}
Let $p\colon\mathcal{E}\to\mathcal{D}$ be a cartesian fibration. Show that the canonical map $\mathcal{E}_d\to\mathcal{E}_{d/}$
admits a right adjoint. Deduce that for a cartesian fibration, the canonical map $\mathcal{E}_d\to\mathcal{E}_{d/}$
is a weak equivalence.

\begin{proof}
Write $\mathcal{E}_{d/}=\mathcal{D}_{d/}\times_{\mathcal{D}}\mathcal{E}$
and denote the inclusion by $i$.
For $t=(\alpha\colon d\to p(e),e)$, choose a $p$-cartesian lift
$\chi\colon\alpha^*e\to e$ of $\alpha$.
Its source lies in $\mathcal{E}_d$.
The pair $(s_0\alpha,\chi)$ defines an edge
$\epsilon_t\colon i(\alpha^*e)\to t$ in $\mathcal{E}_{d/}$:
the triangle $s_0\alpha$ has successive edges $\mathrm{id}_d,\alpha$
and long edge $\alpha$.

Since $p$ is an isofibration by Lemma~3.1.21, the pullback description
of mapping spaces gives, for $c\in\mathcal{E}_d$,
\[
 \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{E}_{d/}}(ic,t)
 \simeq
 \operatorname{hofib}_{\alpha}\bigl(
   \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{E}}(c,e)
   \longrightarrow\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}}(d,p(e))\bigr).
\]
Indeed, its mapping space is the homotopy pullback with
$\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{D}_{d/}}(\mathrm{id}_d,\alpha)$.
This latter space is contractible since $\mathrm{id}_d$ is initial
(the dual of Exercise~\ref{ex:135}),
and its preferred point $s_0\alpha$ projects to $\alpha$.
Corollary~3.1.19 identifies this homotopy fibre with
$\mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{E}_d}(c,\alpha^*e)$ by postcomposition with
$\chi$.
Under the displayed identification, the resulting equivalence is
precisely
\[
 \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{E}_d}(c,\alpha^*e)
 \xrightarrow{\ i\ }
 \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{E}_{d/}}(ic,i(\alpha^*e))
 \xrightarrow{\ \epsilon_t\circ-\ }
 \mathrm{map}_{\mathcal{E}_{d/}}(ic,t).
\]
Proposition~5.1.10 therefore supplies a right adjoint $r$ to $i$,
with $r(t)=\alpha^*e$.

The unit $\mathrm{id}\to ri$ and counit $ir\to\mathrm{id}$ realize
to topological homotopies by Remark~1.2.60.
Thus $|r|$ is a homotopy inverse to $|i|$, proving the required
weak equivalence.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
