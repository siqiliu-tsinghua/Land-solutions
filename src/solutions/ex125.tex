\begin{exercise}{125}
Show that the functor $(-)^{\flat}\colon\mathrm{sSet}\to\mathrm{sSet}^{+}$ sends Joyal equivalences to
marked equivalences.

\begin{proof}
For every simplicial set $K$ and $\infty$-category $\mathcal C$, every
functor $K\to\mathcal C$ sends the marked edges of $K^{\flat}$ to
degenerate edges, hence to equivalences.
Thus Definition~3.2.14 gives a natural equality of full subcategories
\[
 \mathrm{Fun}^{\mathrm{mcc}}(K^{\flat},\mathcal C)
 =\mathrm{Fun}(K,\mathcal C).
\]
If $g\colon X\to Y$ is a Joyal equivalence, Definition~2.2.9 makes
$g^*\colon\mathrm{Fun}(Y,\mathcal C)\to\mathrm{Fun}(X,\mathcal C)$
an equivalence for every $\mathcal C$.
Under these equalities it is exactly the restriction map induced by $g^{\flat}$.
Definition~3.2.20 therefore makes $g^{\flat}$ a marked equivalence.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
