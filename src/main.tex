\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}
This volume is a companion to Markus Land's
\emph{Introduction to Infinity-Categories} (2021).
Exercise statements retain the wording, numbering, and mathematical
layout of the book, with corrections explained in footnotes.
References to numbered
definitions, lemmas, propositions, and theorems refer to the book unless
stated otherwise.
This volume incorporates errata from the translator's notes to a separate
Chinese translation of Land's book, together with additional corrections
to exercise statements justified in the local footnotes.

This draft contains solutions to Exercises~1--80. Products of maps are
composed from right to left, and $\alpha^*$ denotes $X(\alpha)$ for a
simplicial set $X$.
