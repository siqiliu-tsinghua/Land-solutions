\begin{exercise}{146}
Show that left-anodyne maps do not satisfy the left cancellation property
among monomorphisms.

\begin{proof}
Consider the inclusions
\[
 \{1\}\xrightarrow{f}\Delta^1\xrightarrow{g}J,
\]
where $g$ is the generating edge $0\to1$.
In the filtration from the proof of Exercise~\ref{ex:76},
$K_1=\Delta^1$ and the tail $K_1\to J$ is built from left horn
attachments. Hence $g$ is left-anodyne.
That exercise also makes $\{0\}\to J$ left-anodyne;
interchanging the two objects of $J$ shows that
$gf\colon\{1\}\to J$ is left-anodyne.

Every left horn contains vertex $0$, so a simplex of $\Delta^1$
whose left horn lies in $\{1\}$ starts at $1$ and is constant.
Thus $f$ is a left fibration.
Its square against itself with identity horizontal maps has no lift,
so $f$ is not left-anodyne.
All three maps are monomorphisms, and $g,gf$ are left-anodyne
while $f$ is not, disproving left cancellation.
\end{proof}
\end{exercise}
